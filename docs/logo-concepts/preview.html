Two chevron-like shapes converging at a central point — one in accent indigo, one lighter. The negative space forms an abstract K. Suggests Plan + Execution converging into a verified outcome. Pure straight lines, no thin strokes — scales cleanly to 16px.

A hexagonal prism with a K carved out as negative space. The K is formed by the background showing through — works on any theme. Most "techy / futuristic" of the three; leans hardest on geometric precision, which matches the verification theme. Note: at 16px the negative-space K is harder to read than concepts 1 and 2 — flag if you care more about small-size legibility than concept distinctiveness.
